# Donut Dash 🍩

A phone-tilt obstacle course game for kids. Steer a donut down a ramp by tilting your phone left and right. Tap to jump over gaps, land on moving platforms, collect sprinkles, and hit frosting boost pads to reach the finish line.

**[Play now](https://ashleywolf.github.io/donut-dash/)**

## How to Play

**On mobile (recommended):**
- Tilt your phone left/right to steer the donut
- Tap anywhere to jump
- Collect sprinkles for points
- Roll over blue boost pads for a speed burst
- Reach the checkered flag to win

**On desktop:**
- Arrow keys or A/D to move
- Space or Up arrow to jump

## Features

- Phone accelerometer controls (DeviceOrientation API)
- Procedurally generated obstacle course with increasing difficulty
- Gaps, moving platforms, ramps, hills, speed boosts, and collectibles
- Score tracking with local best score
- Sound effects via Web Audio API
- Single HTML file, no dependencies
